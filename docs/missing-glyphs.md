## Collapsed Punctuation

- `ellipsis` (U+2026, …): three separate dots restored in the candidate.
- `quotedblbase` (U+201E, „): two separate comma forms restored in the candidate.

All other glyph outlines, advances, variation data, and kerning are unchanged.

The Latin Core classification uses the official glyphset:
https://github.com/googlefonts/glyphsets/blob/main/Lib/glyphsets/results/nam/GF_Latin_Core.nam
